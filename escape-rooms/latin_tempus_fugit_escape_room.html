A Latin escape room · present & imperfect tense

Solve each room to win a golden letter.

You step into a quiet Roman villa. In the atrium stands a great water clock, but the water hangs frozen in mid-drip. Time has stopped.

On the wall, someone has scratched a message:

"Time froze because the tenses got tangled. Untangle the now from the back then, gather the five golden letters, and speak the magic word at the Door of Time."

Room I · Ātrium

The Ending Mosaic

Six mosaic tiles have fallen from the floor. Match each pronoun to the verb ending that belongs to it. Tap a pronoun, then tap its ending.

Room II · Tablīnum

Whose Action Is It?

Scrolls lie scattered on the desk, each with one verb. The ending tells you who is acting. Pick the hidden subject.

Room III · Hōrologium

Now or Back Then?

The clock room. Verbs swirl in the frozen air. Send each one to its own time: NUNC if it happens now (present), TUNC if it was going on back then (imperfect, the -bā- verbs).

Room IV · Officīna

The Verb Forge

A workbench with three empty molds. Forge each imperfect verb from its three parts: stem + bā + ending. Tap the tiles in order.

Room V · Bibliothēca

The Translation Locks

Three locked chests. Each opens only for the true translation.

Porta Temporis

The Door of Time

Five golden letters glow in your hand. Arrange them into the magic word and time will flow again.

Hint: Tempus ________ … "time flies!"

TEMPVS FVGIT!

The water drips, the clock turns, time flies once more.

Present = happening now → -ō, -s, -t, -mus, -tis, -nt

Imperfect = going on back then → stem + bā + m, s, t, mus, tis, nt

-bā- is your time machine: it always means "was / were …-ing" or "used to".
